Privacy notice

Last updated 10 November 2026 · 3 minute read

On this page What we collect What stays local Sharing Your choices Contact

What we collect

Almost nothing. Acrostia is a static website: there is no account system, no database of names, and no sign-up form. When you load the site we receive the standard technical request logs any web server receives, such as your browser type and the page requested.

What stays local

Every name you type stays in your browser. Poems are generated on your device, and nothing you type is transmitted to us or to any third party. If you close the tab, it is gone.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any to begin with. Share links you create contain only the poem data you chose to put in them.

Your choices

You can use the entire site without accepting analytics cookies. See the cookie notice for what the optional toggle controls and how to switch it off.

Contact

Questions about this notice can go to the address on the contact page. A person replies.
