No tracking cookies

Acrostia sets no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is no cookie to consent to.

What is stored

One item is kept in your browser local storage: the date you last entered on a birthday page, so the next page can show the same reading. It has no identifier attached and never leaves your device.

Analytics

The cookieless counter built into the site is switched off. When on, it counts page views without cookies or personal data, which is why it needs no consent.

Clearing it

Clear site data for this domain in your browser settings and the stored date goes with it. The site keeps working exactly as before.
