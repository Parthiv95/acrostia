Your Chinese zodiac animal

Your Chinese zodiac animal is decided by the Lunar New Year, not by January 1, so people born in January or February often get the wrong sign online. This page checks the real lunar boundary for every year from 1900 to 2030.

Why the exact date matters

The twelve animals repeat every lunar year, and each lunar year starts on the date of the new moon that begins it, somewhere between January 21 and February 20. A child born on January 26, 1990 is a Snake even though 1990 is called a Horse year online, because the Horse year did not begin until January 27.

Most calculators skip that step and just subtract. This page keeps a verified new moon table for every year from 1900 to 2030 and compares your exact date against it, so the sign you get matches the lunar calendar.
