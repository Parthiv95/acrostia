A cake with their name on it

Nothing says happy birthday like a cake with the right name on it. Bakeries charge extra for the piping; here it is free and takes two seconds. Type the name, watch the icing spell it out letter by letter, then download the cake and send it to whoever is blowing out the candles this week.

The name is piped onto a real chocolate ganache cake in smooth cream icing, sized to fit: long names get written a little smaller, short names get the big script. A small "Happy Birthday" goes in above it, just like a bakery would pipe it. If you also want words to go with the cake, the Acrostia studio writes a birthday poem for any name.
