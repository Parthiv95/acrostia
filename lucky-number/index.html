Where lucky numbers come from

Numerology reads luck from two places: your birth date and your name. The birth number reduces the day you were born to a single digit. The life path reduces the whole date. Your zodiac sign adds its own lucky color, day and number, and your name letters add a final flourish. Acrostia combines all of them and hands you one card.

The Lucky pick of the day is the one playful exception: it rolls fresh each time you tap, which is exactly what a lucky number is for. When you want the poem version of your luck, the studio is one tap away.
