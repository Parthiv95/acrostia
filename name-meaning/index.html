Your name, spelled out in meaning

A name is the first gift anyone receives, and like most gifts it comes without instructions. A name meaning reading writes the instructions: each letter of the name starts one line, and each line names something true about the person carrying it. Emma reads as Energetic, Marvelous, Merry and Able. Liam reads as Loyal, Inspiring, Able and Motivated.

E is for Energetic, full of life and spirit
M is for Marvelous, wonderful in every way
M is for Merry, cheer that never quits
A is for Able, potential to achieve big

The reading also looks at the shape of the name: how many letters, how many vowels, where the weight falls. Names rich in vowels read as feelers. Names packed with consonants read as doers. Short names rarely need a second introduction; long names tend to come with stories attached.

Questions people ask

Is the reading the same every time?
Yes. The same name always gives the same reading, so you can compare names, share yours, and send friends to check theirs.
Can I turn the reading into a poem?
Yes. The Acrostia studio turns any name into a full poem on a themed card you can download, print or send on WhatsApp.
Does it work for any name in the world?
Any name spelled with the 26 English letters works, from Aarav to Yuki. Names with accents are matched to their closest letter.
