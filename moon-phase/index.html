The moon phase on your birthday

Every birthday has a moon. Type yours and see the exact phase in the sky the night you were born, drawn to scale, with the personality the old moon lore gives it.

Why your moon phase matters

The moon cycles through eight faces every 29 and a half days, and people have attached a meaning to each one for as long as there have been calendars. Born under a full moon is the showstopper of the bunch. Born under a waning crescent is the quiet one. None of it is science, all of it is delightful.

The drawing on your card is the real phase for your date and year, so it works for any birthday back to 1900. Screenshot it for a profile, or pair it with the poem side of Acrostia: a name poem under the same moon.
