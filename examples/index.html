The archive

Twelve poems, made by the studio.

Real output, not samples written for the brochure. Each one was generated from a name and an occasion, then left exactly as it came out. The names most people ask for, in the order the world is naming its children right now.

Olivia
Acrostic · birthday
  1. Open-hearted, first to welcome a stranger in,
  2. Laughs at her own jokes before anyone else does,
  3. Insists on the small ceremonies, the candles, the cake,
  4. Very hard to argue with, impossible to replace,
  5. In every room she is the one who remembers,
  6. And the year is better for having her in it.
