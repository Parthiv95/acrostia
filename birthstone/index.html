What your birthstone means

The birthstone tradition goes back to the twelve stones on the breastplate of Aaron, one per tribe, which later settled into one gem per month. Each stone carries a story: garnet for safe travel, sapphire for truth, pearl for patience, because it is the only gem a living thing makes.

Pick a month and Acrostia hands you the stone and its one-line story, plus the whole twelve-stone wheel to browse. Birthstones make a gift feel designed rather than bought: a garnet for a January friend says you looked it up, which is the point.
