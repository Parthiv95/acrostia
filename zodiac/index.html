What your zodiac sign says

Your zodiac sign comes from where the sun sat on the sky map the moment you were born. Each of the twelve signs carries an element (fire, earth, air or water) and a ruling planet, and together they sketch a personality: the starter, the builder, the talker, the feeler, and so on around the wheel.

Acrostia keeps it friendly: pick a birthday, get the sign, its glyph, its dates, its element and planet, its line in plain words, and the lucky color, day and number that go with it. Tap any sign in the grid to compare with friends. And when you want the poem version, the studio turns any name into a card.
