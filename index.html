Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Every poem, every background, every download. There is no account, no trial and no card hidden behind a paywall.

Do you store the names I type?

No. The poem is written by your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so there is nothing stored.

Can I print the cards at home?

Yes. Use the Print button for a clean greeting-card print, or download the picture and send it to any photo printer.

Which occasions are covered?

51 of them, from birthdays, weddings and new jobs to Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Hanukkah, Lunar New Year, Holi, Rakhi and Thanksgiving.

Can I make a poem for a couple?

Yes. Weddings, engagements, anniversaries, Valentine's and couple categories take two names and weave both into the acrostic.

Can I use the poems for my business?

The poems and cards are yours to share, gift and print freely. All we ask is that the little "made with Acrostia" line stays on.
