What your birth flower means

Every month has a flower, the way every month has a stone. The tradition is old, floriography, the Victorian language of flowers, gave each bloom a meaning, and the birth flower calendar gathers them: carnations for January, roses for June, marigolds for October, poinsettias for December.

Pick a month and see the flower with its line, then browse the whole year. A birth flower is a lovely thing to put on a card, which is exactly what the Acrostia studio is for.
