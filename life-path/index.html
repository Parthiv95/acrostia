Your life path number

The life path number is the total of every digit in your birth date, reduced to a single digit, or kept as a master number 11, 22 or 33 when the sum lands on one. It is the headline number of numerology.

What is a life path number

Numerology takes the month, day and year of birth, adds every digit, and keeps reducing until one number is left. That number, the life path, is treated as the broad shape of a person\u2019s road: 1 the starter, 7 the questioner, 9 the old soul at the end of the row.

There is one famous detour: when the sum lands on 11, 22 or 33 before a final reduction, numerology keeps it as a master number instead of boiling it down. This page shows the whole trail digit by digit, so you can see exactly where your date landed. The Birthday Ledger keeps it beside your birth number and the rest of your reading.
