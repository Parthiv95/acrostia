Your age on other planets

One trip around the Sun is a year on Earth and nowhere else. Type your birthday and find how young you really are on Mercury, and how impossibly old on Neptune.

Why your space age changes so much

A birthday is one full orbit of the Sun. Mercury finishes an orbit in 88 Earth days, so a 30 year old Earthling is over 125 in Mercury years. Neptune needs 165 Earth years per lap, so nobody reading this has had a single Neptunian birthday yet unless they are very patient or very fictional.

Your card also counts down the next birthday on each world, measured in Earth days, which is the fun part: Mercury birthdays come around about four times a year. When you are done visiting the planets, the studio writes poems back on Earth.
