The Birthday Ledger

Your golden birthday, found

The golden birthday is the one where the age matches the date: turning 24 on the 24th. It comes around exactly once, and most people miss theirs without ever knowing. Find yours, plus the day counts nobody thinks to check.

What exactly is a golden birthday

The rule is simple: if you were born on the 9th, your golden birthday is your 9th. Born on the 24th? It is a long wait to 24, and if you were born on the 31st the party is legendary. The idea started in American party culture and stuck because it turns an ordinary year into a once-ever event.

Missed yours? The card also finds your 10,000th day on Earth, which lands in your late twenties and makes a far better surprise party theme than another round-number birthday. And when it is time for the card, the studio writes the poem.
