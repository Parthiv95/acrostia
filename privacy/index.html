Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version: Acrostia runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and nothing you type is sent to or stored on our servers.

What we collect

Nothing. Every poem is generated on your device with JavaScript. The names, occasions and styles you pick never leave your browser. If you share a poem link, the details ride in the link itself, visible to whoever you send it to, exactly like a message you wrote yourself.

The contact form

The "Say hello" form does not submit data anywhere. It opens your own email app with the text prefilled, and you choose whether to send it. If you do, your email reaches parthivkk123@gmail.com and is handled like any personal email. Ask us to delete correspondence at any time and we will.

What is stored on your device

One thing only: your answer to the cookie notice at the bottom of the page, kept in your browser's local storage under the key acrostia-consent, so the notice stays closed. No site can read another site's storage.

Third parties we load from

  • Google Fonts: the Fraunces, Onest, Great Vibes, Caveat and Playfair Display typefaces, served from fonts.googleapis.com.
  • Unsplash and Pexels: the example photos on the studio, fun tools and backgrounds pages, served from their image CDNs.

Requesting these resources exposes your IP address, browser type and the page you are on to those providers, as with any image or font on any website. No tracking parameters, identifiers or referrer tricks are added by us.

Children

Acrostia is not directed at children under 13, and since no data is collected there is nothing for a child to consent to. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, write to us at the address below.

Emails and unsubscribe

We send no newsletters and no email of any kind, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

Deleting your data

There is no server-side data about you to delete. To remove the one local item (the cookie notice choice), clear site data for this site in your browser settings.

Who runs Acrostia

Acrostia is built and operated by Parthiv K, India. Questions, requests or complaints: parthivkk123@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
