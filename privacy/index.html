Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: we collect nothing. The whole studio runs in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Names, partner names, the From line and any photo you upload never leave your device. The poem is written by your own browser, and the download is created on your own device.

Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies and run no trackers. If analytics are added later, this page will be updated before that happens.

Third party services

The page loads two typefaces from Google Fonts and, on the home page, photographs hosted by Unsplash. Those services see a standard request from your browser, such as your IP address, when the font or image is fetched. Sharing to WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with your poem text; that happens entirely on your device.

Your uploads

Photos you choose as a card background are read locally to draw the card. They are never uploaded anywhere by us.

Contact

Use the Say hello form on the home page. It opens your own email app with a draft, so the message travels through your email provider, not through us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change will appear on this page with a new date.
