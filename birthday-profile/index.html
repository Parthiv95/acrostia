What a birthday profile tells you

Your birth date carries more than your age in years, months and days. The same date names your Western zodiac sign and its ruling planet, your Chinese zodiac animal, the birthstone and birth flower and birth tree of your month, the weekday you were born on, and a small stack of lucky things: your color, your day, your number. A birthday profile lines them all up in one place.

Everything here is calculated from the date alone, so the same birthday always gives the same profile, and you can compare friends, family and famous birthdays without signing up for anything. When you are done, download the profile card and send it in a chat, or create a share link. The link carries the name and birth date you typed and is built only when you ask for it.

Questions people ask

Is the profile the same every time?
Yes. Every fact is derived from the birth date itself, so the same date always gives the same profile. Tap Surprise me if you want a random date to explore.
Does the year matter?
Yes, for your exact age, your weekday, your leap years count and your Chinese zodiac animal. The zodiac sign, birthstone, flower and tree depend on the day and month.
Can I share it?
Yes, and sharing stays in your hands. Tap Create share link to see the exact link first: it carries the name and birth date you entered, so anyone who opens it sees the same profile prefilled. Copy it or open WhatsApp only when you are ready. You can also download the profile card as a picture.
